    About the model

    A generic modern alto in E♭. Written pitch is shown; the instrument sounds a major sixth lower. The first open tone hole sets the note, and the octave key opens one of two small register holes. The parts are larger than true scale where that helps to see them, and the key travel is exaggerated.

    Facts from J. Wolfe (UNSW Music Acoustics), S. Howard's repair notes on octave mechanisms, Taming the Saxophone, public-domain US patents, and other repair notes. The full list, with the parts that are our own inference, is in CONTRACT.md. Our own model and text.

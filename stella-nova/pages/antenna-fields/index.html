Equations

The field of each short piece of current, with all three terms. The page adds these up for every pixel.

The picture at time t is the real part of the phasor.

Currents that nobody feeds (the Yagi-Uda reflector and directors) come from the moment method: the field of all the currents must be zero along each wire.

About

Close to the wire the field stores energy and gives it back each cycle. Farther out, closed loops of electric field break away and travel outward at the speed of light. That is radiation.

The field lines are exact where a flux function exists (a dipole seen from the side, a small loop, any of these antennas seen from above). In the other views the page traces the field direction.

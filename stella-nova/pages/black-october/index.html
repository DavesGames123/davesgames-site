Black October

How to read this

  • The distance from the centre is time. Each thin ring is one UTC day, and each day has the same width. The shaded band in the middle holds the events before the window, in time order but not to scale, with a dashed ring at each month.
  • Each wedge is one thread: one incident and the events that follow from it. A line joins an event to the event it follows from, and always runs outward in time.
  • Filled: confirmed, a primary source states it. Hollow: reported, news only. Slashed: disputed, the sources disagree or the claim is denied.
  • A bigger dot has more sources. The first event of a thread is a little bigger.
  • A dashed arc is a link between two threads that a source makes.
  • Point at a dot, or tap it, for its record and every source. Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom. Press play to watch the record grow, one event at a time.

# Black Hole: credits and licences

## Artwork

The Milky Way background (mode 3, "Milky Way") is a real photograph. It
replaces the procedural nebula in that slot. The shader still draws the
nebula while the photo downloads, or when the download fails.

| File | Work | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `artwork/eso-milky-way-2048.jpg` | Serge Brunier for ESO, *The Milky Way panorama* (ESO image eso0932a, GigaGalaxy Zoom, released 14 Sep 2009). Credit: ESO/S. Brunier. Changes: the 4000 x 2000 publication JPEG resized to 2048 x 1024 and saved again as JPEG, quality 80. The page bends it through the ray tracer. | https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/ (file https://cdn.eso.org/images/publicationjpg/eso0932a.jpg) | CC BY 4.0, https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/ (read 2026-10-18) |

ESO asks for the full credit next to the image. The page shows
"Milky Way: ESO/S. Brunier · CC BY 4.0 · resized" (`#bgCredit`, a link to
the ESO page) while mode 3 is on.

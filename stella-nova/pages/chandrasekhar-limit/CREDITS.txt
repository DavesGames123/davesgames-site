CHANDRASEKHAR LIMIT · image credits and licences
=================================================

Every image in img/ is a resized copy (JPEG, metadata removed) of an
official release. The credit line is copied unchanged from the source
page, as the licences ask. The page shows the same credit in each caption.

  https://chandra.harvard.edu/photo/image_use.html

# Chladni Plate: credits and licences

## Artwork

The two engraved plates in the "Chladni's own figures" block of the
panel are real figures that Ernst Chladni drew from his own sand
experiments. The plate model, the sand and the renderer are this site's
own code and use nothing from the engravings.

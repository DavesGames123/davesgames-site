Circular polarization

The electric field turns as the wave moves. Its tip draws a helix along the ray, and at one point in space it draws a circle in time.

Field

Two components at right angles, out of phase by δ. Amplitude falls as 1/r. The one-ray view draws r·E, so the helix keeps its size.

B is at right angles to E and to the ray, and in phase with it.

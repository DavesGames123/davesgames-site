City Atlas data: sources, licences and credits
================================================

The files <id>.bin in this folder are built by ../tools/build_city.py from
the open datasets below. Each file holds, for one city: building footprints
and heights, water and land cover rasters, ground and sea-floor heights,
two days of surface currents, and the 2025 wind rose.

Buildings, water polygons and place names
  Overture Maps Foundation, release 2026-09-23.1 (themes: buildings, base
  water, divisions). https://overturemaps.org/
  Licence: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0,
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  Attribution: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Overture Maps Foundation,
  and the other sources that Overture lists for these themes:
  https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/
  The building, water and label data in the .bin files are a Derivative
  Database of that data. They are made available under ODbL 1.0, the same
  licence. The build scripts in ../tools/ are the means to make them again.

Surface currents
  Navy ESPC-D-V02 global HYCOM 1/12 degree analysis, hourly surface
  velocity (ssu, ssv), 24-26 September 2026, from HYCOM.org
  (https://www.hycom.org/dataserver/espc-d-v02). U.S. Navy / FNMOC data,
  distributed by HYCOM.org as freely available.
  NOAA Operational Forecast Systems (NYOFS, SFBOFS, SSCOFS), through the
  Stella Nova Tidal Currents datasets (../../tidal-currents/data/).
  NOAA National Ocean Service, CO-OPS. U.S. Government work, public domain.
  Marine Institute Northeast Atlantic model (NEATL), through the same
  Tidal Currents dataset for Dublin. (c) Irish Marine Institute, CC BY 4.0,
  https://erddap.marine.ie/erddap/griddap/IMI_NEATL.html
  Changes: hourly surface currents resampled to this page's grids, faded
  to zero at the coast, quantized to 8 bits.

Wind
  Default wind (the 2025 wind rose): ERA5 reanalysis through the
  Open-Meteo historical weather API, https://open-meteo.com/
  Live wind on the page: the Open-Meteo forecast API.
  Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0.

Idea
  GeoLibre by Qiusheng Wu (opengeos), MIT licence,
  https://github.com/opengeos/GeoLibre - an open geospatial app that shows
  open building footprints and terrarium terrain in the browser. City Atlas
  follows the same idea with its own WebGPU renderer and uses no GeoLibre
  code.

Renderer, wind solver, streak lines and page design: Dave, for Stella Nova.

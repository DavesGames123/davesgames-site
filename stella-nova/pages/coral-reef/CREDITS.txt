Coral Reef Growth (stella-nova/pages/coral-reef) - credits and licences
=====================================================================

The page code (reef.js, colour.js, saver.js, scene.js, diagram.js,
main.js, index.html, style.css, tests.mjs) is our own code. The coral
species are hooks that we wrote for the engine below.


1. Growth engine: dlaf
----------------------
lib/dla-engine.js is a JavaScript port of the Model class of dlaf
(diffusion-limited aggregation) by Michael Fogleman.
Upstream: https://github.com/fogleman/dlaf
The page does not fetch anything from GitHub. The port is a local file.

3. Ideas (no code taken)
------------------------
- J. A. Kaandorp, C. P. Lowe, D. Frenkel and P. M. A. Sloot, "Effect of
  nutrient diffusion and flow on coral morphology", Physical Review
  Letters 77, 2328 (1996). The view of a coral as nutrient-limited
  diffusion-limited aggregation in a flow.
- J. A. Kaandorp and J. E. Kubler, "The Algorithmic Beauty of Seaweeds,
  Sponges and Corals", Springer (2001). Accretive growth: the brain coral
  hook lays each new polyp on the surface of a compact dome.
- T. A. Witten and L. M. Sander, "Diffusion-limited aggregation, a
  kinetic critical phenomenon", Physical Review Letters 47, 1400 (1981).

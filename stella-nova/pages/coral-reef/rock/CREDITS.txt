Coral reef rock (stella-nova/pages/coral-reef/rock and assets/tex/rock)
======================================================================

The rock code (rock/index.js, geology.js, mesh.js, noise.js, material.js,
worker.js) is our own. The gradient noise follows Ken Perlin's improved
noise (2002); the distance transform follows Felzenszwalb and
Huttenlocher, "Distance Transforms of Sampled Functions" (2012); the
smooth minimum and the surface nets idea are well known methods (Inigo
Quilez; Gibson 1998). We wrote the code from the published ideas.

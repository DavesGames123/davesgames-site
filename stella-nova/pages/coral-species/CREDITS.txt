Coral Species (stella-nova/pages/coral-species) - credits and licences
======================================================================

The page code (base.js, species.js, worker.js, main.js, index.html,
style.css, tests.mjs) is our own code. It imports, read only, the coral
reef growth page of this site (stella-nova/pages/coral-reef: reef.js,
coral-sdf.js, mesh-worker.js, colour.js, scene.js, shaders.js, water.js,
layout.js, budget.js, saver.js, crash.js, coral-link.js), which is our
own code too, and stella-nova/lib/dla-sdf.js, lib/action-camera.js and
lib/growth-gradients.js.


1. Growth engine: dlaf
----------------------
lib/dla-engine.js is a JavaScript port of the Model class of dlaf
(diffusion-limited aggregation) by Michael Fogleman.
Upstream: https://github.com/fogleman/dlaf
The page does not fetch anything from GitHub. The port is a local file.

3. Seabed: NOAA NCEI lidar bathymetry (seabed-data.js, v2 only)
-----------------------------------------------------
Source: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI),
Continuously Updated Digital Elevation Model (CUDEM), 1/9 arc-second
topobathy tile ncei19_n18x00_w064x75_2019v1 (St. Croix, US Virgin
Islands). DOI 10.25921/ds9v-ky35 (on 2026-10-17 it redirects to an NCEI page).
Read on 2026-10-17 with OPeNDAP:
  https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/thredds/dodsC/tiles/tiled_19as/ncei19_n18x00_w064x75_2019v1.nc
  variable z, lat index 1314..1473, lon index 4224..4383
  (17.7904 N to 17.7953 N, 64.6198 W to 64.6149 W)
The place is the lagoon of Buck Island Reef National Monument: sand with
patch reefs and the back-reef ridge. seabed-data.js stores the 160 x 160
depths in centimetres. terrain.js scales the relief to the reef (the
scene is not to scale) and adds procedural ripples, rubble and rocks.
Since v3 (the ROV dive) the page does not draw this seabed: the reef
grows on the seeded rock of rock/. terrain.js and seabed-data.js stay
as a compatibility layer until no file imports them.
Licence: a work of the US Government (NOAA). It is not subject to
copyright in the United States (public domain). The NCEI coastal DEM
metadata states: "Produced by the NOAA National Geophysical Data Center.
Not subject to copyright protection within the United States." It is not
for navigation.

5. Code ideas with credit
-------------------------
- lib/dla-sdf.js: the round cone distance and the polynomial smooth
  minimum follow the formulas of Inigo Quilez (iquilezles.org articles
  "distance functions" and "smooth minimum"; MIT). Surface nets after
  S. F. F. Gibson, "Constrained elastic surface nets" (1998). Our code.
- shaders.js and water.js: the caustic map uses the mesh-area method of
  our own page stella-nova/pages/photon-caustics (topview.js), on a
  random sea of 28 waves (swell, wind sea, chop; deep-water dispersion
  omega = sqrt(g k)), warped and broken into patches by fbm noise.
  The lamp volume uses the Henyey-Greenstein phase function (L. G.
  Henyey and J. L. Greenstein, Astrophysical Journal 93, 70, 1941) and
  the tone map is the ACES fit of K. Narkowicz (2015).
- rov/link.js: the SSTV camera link uses the encoder and decoder of our
  own SSTV page, stella-nova/pages/sstv (codec.js, modes.js,
  session.js), imported, not copied. Its CREDITS.md names the sources
  of the mode timings. Bump mapping from
  screen derivatives after M. Mikkelsen, "Bump mapping unparametrized
  surfaces on the GPU" (2010).

About

The scan and the reconstruction run in your browser: a Joseph cone-beam projector, Beer-Lambert physics with Poisson noise and a polychromatic spectrum, then FDK (Feldkamp, Davis and Kress 1984) on the GPU or SIRT on the CPU. We wrote this code. The real tool for this work is the ASTRA Toolbox (W. van Aarle et al., Ultramicroscopy 2015; Optics Express 2016).

Model objects: we turned surface models into voxels and gave each part a material (bone, steel, glass, resin and so on), so their insides are our choice, not a scan. The walnut and the rabbit are real CT scans. Full credits: CREDITS.md.

Cone-beam scan

A cone of X-rays and a flat detector turn once around a 3D phantom. The FDK method (Feldkamp, Davis and Kress, 1984) filters each 2D projection and back-projects it on the GPU. The colour map (Controls, Colour maps, 3D) colours the MIP, the slices and the volume rendering.

About this lab

All the code on this page is our own: the Joseph projector, filtered back-projection, ART, SART, SIRT and CGLS, fan beam and the X-ray physics are textbook methods. The real tool for this work is the ASTRA Toolbox (GPL-3.0), which inspired the lab. We did not copy or port its code.

W. van Aarle et al., The ASTRA Toolbox: a platform for advanced algorithm development in electron tomography, Ultramicroscopy 157 (2015) 35-47, doi:10.1016/j.ultramic.2015.05.002.

W. van Aarle et al., Fast and flexible X-ray tomography using the ASTRA toolbox, Optics Express 24(22) (2016) 25129-25147, doi:10.1364/OE.24.025129.

Shepp-Logan is the published 1974 ellipse table. The other objects are procedural. Attenuation values are approximate, at 70 keV. HU = 1000 (μ − μwater) / μwater.

'safe' is the start after a session that ended uncleanly while zoomed in (localStorage flag dla-terrain-crash-guard, cleared on pagehide and a hidden tab): 2 Mpx, 1 Mpx near, 1024 x 2 shadows, governor level 1 at least (no POM, no vertex relief, one cascade), screen shafts, 128 px impostors; a notice says so; ?gpu=safari or ?gpu=desktop undoes it. gpu.mem = { live, peak, count, byLabel } counts every createBuffer and createTexture of every module, less destroy(). A profile id test for a phone must be /^phone/, not !== 'desktop'. On every profile: at most 1 frame in flight while the last GPU time was over half the heavy limit, and a frame not done after 4 s frees its slot and counts as heavy. fg.ready() false: main.js skips the module updates and queue writes of that frame (frame.dt then holds the skipped time). The real modules showed no GPU byte growth and no allocation per frame in a 200-step zoom loop on a byte-counting mock (jsdom); a near change rebuilds the screen targets (11 builds in 10 zoom cycles). Not measured: real Safari GPU times and memory; no browser ran.

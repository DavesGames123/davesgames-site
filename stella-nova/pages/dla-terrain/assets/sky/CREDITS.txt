DLA Terrain night sky (package CELESTIAL): data and image credits
==================================================================
Every file in this folder, its source, its licence and the source URL.
The files were made by tools in the scratchpad (resize, webp, packing);
the build steps are in the line of each file. tests/celestial.test.mjs
checks that each file in this folder has a line here.

stars.bin
  The Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised Ed. (Hoffleit D., Warren Jr W.H.,
  Astronomical Data Center, NSSDC/ADC, 1991), VizieR catalogue V/50.
  J2000 positions, V magnitudes and B-V colours of 9096 stars, packed to
  10 bytes a star (sky-maps.json "stars").
  Licence: public domain (US government / NASA ADC catalogue data; facts).
  URL: https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/V/50/

milkyway_2k.webp, milkyway_1k.webp
  "Deep Star Maps 2020", milkyway_2020_4k.exr (the sky with the Hipparcos
  and Tycho stars taken out), resized to 2048 x 1024 and 1024 x 512,
  encoded as value^(1/2.2) of the linear map over its 99.99th percentile.
  Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
  Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
  Licence: NASA media, public domain; Gaia DR2 data credited as asked.
  URL: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/

moon_1024.webp, moon_512.webp
  "CGI Moon Kit", lroc_color_poles_2k.tif (LRO LROC WAC colour mosaic).
  Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (Ernie Wright), LRO LROC.
  Licence: NASA media, public domain.
  URL: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

sky-maps.json
  The manifest of this folder (sizes, map means, the star record layout).
  Made by CELESTIAL; no third-party content.

Ideas (no code copied): the B-V to temperature fit of Ballesteros (2012,
EPL 97, 34008); the Planck locus fits of Kim et al. (2002, US patent
7024034); the lunar phase law of Allen, "Astrophysical Quantities"; the
airmass of Kasten and Young (1989, Applied Optics 28, 4735).

      About

      Mountain ranges here grow like frost: diffusion-limited aggregation builds a branching tree, and each level of the tree is blurred and sharpened again, so ridges branch the way real drainage divides do. Branched flow (rays bent by a smooth random field) gathers into channels that cut valleys and deltas, then stream-power erosion, sediment, climate and biomes finish the land.

      Credits

      dlaf by Michael Fogleman, MIT licence: github.com/fogleman/dlaf. lib/dla-engine.js is our JavaScript port of its model.

      Terrain method: the DLA upscale method, branched flow (Heller, Nature 2016), priority flood (Barnes, Lehman and Mulla 2014), stream power (Braun and Willett 2013), deposition (Yuan et al. 2019). Our own code.

      Post: AgX tone map (Troy Sobotka; polynomial fit by Benjamin Wrensch), dual filter bloom (Marius Bjørge 2015), FXAA (Timothy Lottes 2009), TAA variance clip (Salvi 2016).

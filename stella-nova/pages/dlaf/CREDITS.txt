Diffusion-limited aggregation page (stella-nova/pages/dlaf)
===========================================================

This page credits and shows dlaf by Michael Fogleman:

  dlaf - Diffusion-limited aggregation, fast.
  https://github.com/fogleman/dlaf

The growth on this page is lib/dla-engine.js, a JavaScript port of the
Model class of dlaf.cpp. The walk, the join test, the push-back, the five
parameters and the five hooks come from dlaf. The port uses a grid
pyramid in place of the boost R-tree, and takes the hooks as functions.
The page also shows short extracts of dlaf.cpp (AddParticle, ShouldJoin,
RandomStartingPosition and the commented ring of seeds in main()).

The page does not copy the README images of dlaf and makes no network
calls to GitHub.

How the exotic atoms work

Every cloud on this page is a probability density |ψ|² of one state, drawn as tens of thousands of points sampled from it. Notes in each section say what is an exact solution and what is a model.

Hydrogen to n = 300

The bound states of one charge around another are

At n = 300 the factorials in the normalization of R_nl are near 10⁶¹⁴ and overflow a double. The page keeps the normalization as a sum of logarithms and rescales the Laguerre recurrence while it runs. The angular part uses the normalized Legendre recurrence. The checks integrate |R|²r² to 1 at n = 50, 100, 200 and 300. The radial sampler uses a grid that scales with n²a. Exact for a point nucleus without relativity [codata, bethe].

At the top of the ladder, n = 300, the atom is about 10 µm across, the size of a red blood cell, and is bound by 0.15 meV. A neighbouring level is 245 MHz away: a radio photon.

Circular, elliptical and low-l states

For one n, the largest l = |m| = n − 1 gives a thin ring of radius n²a: the circular state [hulet83]. It can decay only one step down, so it lives longest. Low l goes through the nucleus. In an electric field the good quantum numbers are parabolic, n₁, n₂ and m:

The Stark state with the largest |n₁ − n₂| is the elliptical state: its cloud sits on one side of the nucleus with a dipole of (3/2)n(n₁ − n₂) e a. Exact to second order in F [bethe]. When the field passes the Inglis–Teller value

the n and n + 1 manifolds touch and the levels merge [inglis39]. At 10 mV/cm this happens at n ≈ 176.

Wave packets

A short pulse excites many n at once. The sum

moves like the classical electron for a few orbits, spreads round the ring, and comes back together at the revival time [parker86, yeazell88]. The page draws points from the incoherent mixture and weights each point by |Ψ(t)|² over that mixture, so the cloud is the exact density at each time. The circular packet uses circular states; the radial packet uses l = 1 states and breathes in and out.

Alkali Rydberg atoms: quantum defects

A low-l orbit dives into the closed core, sees more of the nuclear charge and is bound more deeply. The defects are fits to measured spectra: Rb [li03, han06], Cs [deig16], Na (as compiled in ARC) [arc] and Sr singlet series [vail12]. Above F, the alkalis use the core-polarization model δ_l = 3α_d / (4(l + 3/2)(l + 1)(l + ½)l(l − ½)). The ground and first P levels use measured term values [nist]. The cloud of an alkali state is drawn as hydrogen at the same n and l (a model: the real cloud differs inside the core); its readouts use n* = n − δ_l.

Positronium, muonic hydrogen, antihydrogen

For two bodies only the reduced mass μ changes the scale: a = a₀ m/μ and E ∝ μ.

  • Positronium, μ = m/2: twice the size, half the binding (6.8 eV). Equal masses with opposite charges give zero orbital magnetic moment, so the page shows no orbital field. S states also annihilate: ortho-positronium lives 142 ns in 1S, longer by n³ above [cassidy18].
  • Muonic hydrogen, μ = 185.8 m: 186 times smaller (285 fm) and 2.53 keV deep, so 1S → 2P is an X-ray. Its Lamb shift measured the proton radius [pohl10]. The muon decays in 2.2 µs.
  • Antihydrogen: by CPT the levels and the cloud are those of hydrogen; ALPHA measured 1S–2S to 2 parts in 10¹² [alpha18]. The positron has charge +e, so the current and the moment reverse.

Rydberg excitons in Cu₂O

An electron and a hole in copper oxide form a hydrogen-like pair with Ry = 92 meV and a = 1.11 nm. Kazimierczuk and co-workers saw P states up to n = 25, more than 2 µm across [kaz14]. The page uses E = E_g − Ry/n² without the small exciton quantum defects and does not compute a field (the moment depends on the effective masses). A model with measured constants.

Trilobite and butterfly molecules

A ground-state atom inside the orbit scatters the Rydberg electron through a contact potential 2πa_s δ(r − R). Inside the degenerate manifold, the bound state is the projection of that delta function [gds00]:

With a_s ≈ −16 a₀ the curve U(R) has about n − 3 wells; the outermost lies near 1.7n². The electron piles up at the perturber, which gives a dipole of thousands of debye. The butterfly uses R′_nl(R), the p-wave state [hgs02]. Low-l molecules were seen first [bend09]; trilobites with kilo-debye dipoles in Cs [booth15]; butterflies in Rb [nied16]. First-order model in a hydrogen manifold.

Hydrogen in a white-dwarf field

In a field B the Hamiltonian gains a paramagnetic and a diamagnetic term (β = B / 2.35 × 10⁵ T):

The diamagnetic term squeezes the cloud across the field into a needle along it. The page minimizes the energy of ψ = exp(−ρ²/4s² − √(ρ²/a² + z²/b²)) on a grid. It reproduces the exact binding energies of Kravchenko et al. to 0.1 % at β = 1 and 2.5 % at β = 1000 [krav96, ruder94]. A variational model of the ground state only. A Rydberg state feels the same squeeze at B ≈ B₀/n³: at n = 50 that is 1.9 T, the quasi-Landau regime [garton69].

The magnetic field of the cloud

The probability current of a state and the field it makes are

For a state with e^{imφ} the current is a nest of coaxial loops. The page puts |ψ|² on a grid of (ρ, z) cells and adds the exact field of each loop (complete elliptic integrals). The orbital moment comes out as μ = −m μ_B for hydrogen, zero for m = 0. The spin layer is the magnetization μ_s|ψ|² with its bound current ∇ × M. On the axis of a circular state the field at the nucleus is close to the loop value B ≈ μ₀μ_B(n − 1)/(2πn⁶a³): about 0.2 T for n = 2, falling as n⁻⁵. For the moving circular packet the page sums Biot–Savart over the weighted points in 3D.

The external field controls apply a uniform B (Zeeman shift (m + g_s m_s)μ_B B, Larmor precession of a tilted cloud at eB/2m, 14 GHz per tesla) and a uniform F (the Stark map above).

Lifetimes and other readouts

Hydrogen radiative lifetimes are exact sums over all dipole channels (2p: 1.60 ns, 3s: 158 ns). Other species scale as 1/μ; alkalis use hydrogen at n* (a model). Blackbody depopulation at 300 K uses Γ = 4α³kT/3n*² [cooke80, gallagher]. Circular hydrogen at 300 K uses the full model of the circular-Rydberg page [pult26].

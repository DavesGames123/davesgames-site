About

Each fish is a set of pen lines, made from a seed. The seed is the Latin name: the same name always gives the same fish. A random name comes from the same syllable tables that the engine uses.

The body is two noise curves. Scales are a clipped mesh, fins are rays clipped by the body, and the head, eye, jaw, barbels and teeth are added on top. The result is plain polylines for a pen plotter.

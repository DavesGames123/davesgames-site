# Fly Duel card art: sources and licences

Every card picture is a crop of a public-domain natural-history plate or painting from Wikimedia Commons.
We checked the licence of each file with the Commons API (prop=imageinfo, extmetadata LicenseShortName, and prop=templates) on 2026-10-11.
Each LicenseShortName is "Public domain" or "CC0" (museum releases: NGA, the Met, the Rijksmuseum). No page carries a CC BY tag. The licence templates are in the table and in art.json.
We cropped each picture to a 4:3 box and resized it to 480 x 360 and 240 x 180 px (JPEG quality 80). The crop box of each picture is in art.json as fractions of the source. For a tall subject (crop note "set on a 4:3 field") we put the whole box on a field of the border colour of the source, with a soft edge.

The card names, the card text, the card frame, the mana glyphs and the card back are our own. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

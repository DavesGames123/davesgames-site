Standing Orders Three Design Pillars

Authoritative · Per Steam Brief
SYS NOMINAL SECT 01

Pillar A●

PILLAR · TACTICS

Tactics, not reflexes

Bullets matter, movement is tuned for decisions, and the tension is claustrophobic, not chaotic. Fights reward positioning, patience, and smart use of the environment over raw reflexes.

Pillar B●

PILLAR · ROLE

Engineer. Soldier. Survivor.

You're not a red shirt. You're a mechanically inclined (but battle-trained) engineer — and the only one who can keep the ship running.

Pillar C●

PILLAR · ENVIRONMENT

Use the environment to your advantage

Weaponize the ship. Trigger hazards and environmental kills — but watch out, because stray bullets from enemy robots can cause problems for you, too.

Tactical Pause v2 SUPERHOT-Like Time Dilation

In Implementation
DILATION SPEED v2 PARTIAL

Doctrine●

SCREENSHOT · TIME-DILATED MOMENT

"Starts very slow motion, but speeds up to about 70% when you decide to act."

Time dilates around player intent. At rest the simulation runs in heavy slow-motion; the moment the player commits to an action — moving, shooting, interacting with a device, or casting an ability — time accelerates to roughly 70% real-speed.

Net effect: planning happens almost-paused; commitment costs you tempo. Action and analysis share the same control surface.

PROFILE 01 / 05

Window

SCREENSHOT · DECOMPRESSION KILL

Three states: Working / Closed Broken Broken-Shielded

When broken, a window "will create a sucking area of effect", then after a couple seconds of that "they will shield themselves and become broken shielded."

STRETCH Wire a window to a power conduit — "if the power [conduit] is destroyed, the window will not shield itself."

Implementation Plans & Stretch InterconnectsPLANNED

  • Window ↔ Conduit wiring Stretch — destroy the linked conduit and the broken window will not shield itself, leaving the suction zone permanent.
  • Doors as enemy hosts Likely Add — a door can be enemy-infected; walk through without clearing it and the door fires an attack at you.
  • Pressurized doors Stretch — wired to an oxygen generator on one side. Unequal pressure disables auto-open. Manual click vents "a short burst of air in one direction that is away from pressure" before becoming shielded or a walkable windy area. Final behavior pending.
  • Oxygen generators TBD — device does not currently exist. Leading theory: reuse the existing ice mechanic.
  • Damaged doors auto-shut Considering — open/closed both take damage; "I wouldn't mind it shutting every time it gets damaged."

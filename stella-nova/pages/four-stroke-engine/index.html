Four-stroke engine

Inline four · 2.0 L · firing order 1-3-4-2

Four strokes. Each cylinder draws in air and fuel, squeezes it, burns it, and pushes the burnt gas out: intake, compression, power, exhaust. That takes two turns of the crank.

Four cylinders. The cylinders take turns, half a turn apart, in the order 1-3-4-2, so one of them is always on its power stroke. The flywheel smooths what is left.

The cut. The block, head, cover and pan are cut on the plane of the bores. The cut faces are drawn in red, the way an engineering section is.

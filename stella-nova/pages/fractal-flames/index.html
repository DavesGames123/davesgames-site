About

A fractal flame is an iterated function system. Each xform is an affine map followed by a weighted sum of non-linear variations. The chaos game moves many points through randomly chosen xforms and counts where they land.

flam3 colours each point by a palette index that each xform pulls toward its own colour, and maps the hit count through a logarithm. That log-density map, with gamma and vibrancy, gives the soft glow. A variable-width filter (density estimation) smooths the sparse areas.

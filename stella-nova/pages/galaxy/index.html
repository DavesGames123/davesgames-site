About

The light is a sum of profiles: a triaxial Sérsic bulge, thin and thick exponential disks, a young disk in the arms, a bar and a faint halo. The arms are a density wave: a log-spiral pattern turns at one speed, the stars at another, so the arms never wind up. Young blue stars and pink HII regions are born in an arm and drift ahead of it.

Dust absorbs blue light more than red, so lanes look brown and stars behind them redden. The diffuse light and dust are ray marched; the brightest stars, clusters and tails are sprites. Interacting pairs come from a restricted three-body run on a parabolic orbit.

Sizes are true to type; dust is a little thicker than real so the lanes read at page size.

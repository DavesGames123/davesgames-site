## Paper and code

The page explains Powell et al. 2025, Nature Astronomy 9, 1714. The page
quotes equations and numbers from the paper and does not copy its figures.
`lens.js`, `model.js`, `floor.js`, `build-floor.mjs`, `main.js`,
`index.html` and `style.css` are this site's own code.

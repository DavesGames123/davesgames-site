Six short lessons on the grid, from the first wall to the resource tree. Every figure uses the game's own module and resource art. Tap them to try each rule.

01

Grid placement

Build outward from the core. A new module must touch the station, and each module obeys the placement tags it carries.

  • Connected. A new module touches the hull on one side.
  • Exterior. Living quarters, thrusters and turrets need one side on open space.
  • Furniture. Hydroponics stand on enclosed floor.
  • Facing. Production modules have an output side. Rotate to choose it.
02

Enclosed rooms

Empty cells that built modules fully surround become enclosed. Enclosed cells are your storage and your floor.

  • Close the wall gaps to seal the room.
  • Enclosed cells glow and start to hold resources.
  • Side-connected enclosed cells form one region. Production modules pull from it and push to it.
03

Decompression

Break a wall that seals a room, and everything stored inside ejects into space.

  • Tap any wall to remove it.
  • If the room opens, the stored resources fly out through the breach.
  • Plan demolition: clear the inside before you take down outer walls.
  • Citizens with the Collect job gather floating items back.
04

Foundry flow

A foundry sits in the wall between two enclosed regions. Its facing side is the output, and the other sides feed it. A citizen with the Smelt job works it, and the foundry takes its recipe from the ore it gets.

  • The gold arrow marks the output side.
  • Pick an ore and tap the input room to stock it.
  • Rotate the foundry to swap the input and output rooms.

05

Crew jobs

Toggle which jobs a citizen will do.

  • The ring colour shows each citizen's present job.
  • With every job off, a citizen idles.
  • Mining needs asteroids. Building needs planned modules.
06

Resource tiers

Mined ore climbs through the tiers into components. Select any resource to trace what it is made from and what it feeds.

HALFTONE  ·  credits and licences
=================================

This page is a WebGPU (WGSL) and JavaScript port of glsl-halftone, with
extensions of its own.

glsl-halftone
  Authors:  stackgl / glslify (Matt DesLauriers, npm package glsl-halftone 1.0.4)
  Source:   https://github.com/glslify/glsl-halftone
            (ported from commit 46120a3f59b5bc1801b49e200a7ec5df86119ab2)
  Licence:  MIT, Copyright (c) 2015 stackgl. The full notice is in
            LICENSE-glsl-halftone.txt.
  Ported here:
    index.glsl  halftone(vec3, vec2, float), halftone(vec3, vec2)
                -> shaders/halftone.wgsl halftone, halftone30
                -> halftone-ref.js screenValues, compose, halftonePixel

The halftone method
  Author:   Stefan Gustavson, Linkoping University. glsl-halftone says that
            "the halftone effect is public domain, by Stefan Gustavson".
  Source:   his WebGL shader tutorial, "Procedural textures in GLSL" /
            halftone: http://webstaff.itn.liu.se/~stegu/webglshadertutorial/shadertutorial.html
            (steps 5 and 9 of that tutorial, bilinear sampling and
            minification, are left to the user by glsl-halftone; this page
            uses a mip chain for minification, see gpu.js).

glsl-aastep (a dependency of glsl-halftone)
  Source:   https://github.com/glslify/glsl-aastep
            (commit 49d59670789be4b9863c3991baa3a58b9bff8c05)
  Licence:  MIT, Copyright (c) 2015 stackgl (in LICENSE-glsl-halftone.txt)
  Ported:   aastep -> shaders/halftone.wgsl aastep (the
            GL_OES_standard_derivatives branch), halftone-ref.js aastep

Simplex noise (glsl-noise simplex/2d, from webgl-noise)
  Author:   Ian McEwan, Ashima Arts (2011). webgl-noise is maintained by
            Stefan Gustavson.
  Source:   https://github.com/hughsk/glsl-noise simplex/2d.glsl
            (commit 7870430c384bd53488ff6fe9a47f8a6f571524c4), a copy of
            https://github.com/ashima/webgl-noise src/noise2D.glsl
  Licence:  MIT, Copyright (C) 2011 by Ashima Arts. The full notice is in
            LICENSE-webgl-noise.txt.
  Ported:   mod289, permute, snoise -> shaders/halftone.wgsl mod289_3,
            mod289_2, permute3, snoise; halftone-ref.js snoise
  Note:     glsl-noise has the 2011 permutation ((x*34.0)+1.0)*x. The later
            webgl-noise has +10.0. The port keeps +1.0, the value that
            glsl-halftone uses.

tests.mjs holds a copy of the upstream GLSL (index.glsl with snoise and
aastep put in, as glslify does) under the same MIT licences. It runs that
GLSL in WebGL as the reference for the WGSL port.

Halftone

Four CMYK dot screens at 15, 75, 0 and 45 degrees, computed for each pixel on the GPU. A WGSL port of glsl-halftone.

Image

You can also drop or paste an image here. It stays in your browser. The camera starts only when you click Camera.

Halftone upstream

Frequency is the number of screen cells across the image height. The noise in each dot edge and on the paper is upstream's: three octaves of simplex noise.

Compare

Wheel or pinch to zoom, drag to move. Each zoom and the loupe compute the dots again at that scale.

About

A WGSL port of glsl-halftone (glslify, MIT), with the simplex noise of Ian McEwan, Ashima Arts (MIT). The method is Stefan Gustavson's (public domain), from his WebGL shader tutorial. Photos: NASA, public domain. See CREDITS.txt.

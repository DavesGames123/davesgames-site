// The game · demo available now

What you'll command

The solar system used to be sprawling with life, but no one saw the Great Cataclysm coming. All systems went dark. Everything and everyone fell apart. Everyone but you. Rebuild the once-vibrant solar system into a new civilization, and reclaim what was lost.

  1. Mine10 ores · real rarity curves
  2. SmeltOre → ingot → alloy → component
  3. BuildGrid stations, module by module
  4. GovernCitizens with friendships and grudges
⏱

Time-dilating astrophysics

Thousands of bodies on real n-body orbits. Dilate and compress the flow of time at will.

Gravity SimHohmann Transfer
▦

Modular stations

Place modules on a crossword-puzzle grid that grows in every direction. Enclose cells to make 64-unit storage bays.

Station PlannerStation GuideControls

A crew with opinions

Citizens mine, haul, build and harvest on their own. They get tired, socialize, and pick who they work with.

Smelt ore to ingots, forge steel, press alloys into reactor cores. 40+ recipes, citizen-operated foundries.

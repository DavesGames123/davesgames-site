Human Skeleton mesh data (data/*.bin.gz, data/manifest.json)

The meshes in this folder are a derived work. build-data.py made them from
these sources:

  Z-Anatomy, the libre 3D atlas of anatomy
    Gauthier Kervyn (design, 3D, anatomy), Marcin Zielinski (add-on)
    https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy  (Startup.blend)
    Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0

  BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS)
    https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
    Licence: CC BY 4.0 (the current DBCLS licence page). Z-Anatomy cites the
    earlier release under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. Z-Anatomy made its bone
    meshes from BodyParts3D. The FMA ids in manifest.json come from the
    BodyParts3D table isa_parts_list_e.txt.

Changes made by build-data.py: the bones were selected and merged (the
manubrium, body and xiphoid process into one sternum), moved into a y-up
frame in metres, decimated, given new normals and a cavity term, quantized
and packed. The articulation lists come from mesh proximity plus a short
curated list. The names, Latin names and facts are written for this page.

Not included: the six auditory ossicles. Z-Anatomy credits a CC BY-NC-SA
ear model from the University of Dundee (after the McGill "3D Ear"), and
that model contains the malleus, incus and stapes. The non-commercial
term is not compatible with this folder, so build-data.py leaves the
ossicles out unless it is given --ossicles.

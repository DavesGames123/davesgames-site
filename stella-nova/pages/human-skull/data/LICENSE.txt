Licence of these files
  The meshes in this folder are adapted material: selected, re-oriented,
  welded, decimated with quadric edge collapse, given new smooth normals,
  baked with ambient occlusion, and quantized (see ../build-data.mjs).
  To honour both source licences, these derived files are shared under
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  Attribute as: "Human skull meshes adapted from BodyParts3D,
  (c) The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA 2.1 JP;
  adaptation by davesgames.io, CC BY-SA 4.0."

  The names in English and Latin and the one-line facts in skull.json are
  original text for this page. FMA ids come from the Foundational Model of
  Anatomy, as listed by BodyParts3D.

About

The skull of one adult man, as it is drawn in BodyParts3D, the open 3D anatomy of the Database Center for Life Science in Japan. Each bone and each tooth is its own model, from CT data.

Anatomy opens the skull along the lines that its parts take. Symmetry puts each left and right pair face to face across the midline. Region sorts the parts into the cranium, the face, the teeth and the hyoid. Catalogue lays every part flat on a tray, largest first.

Pick a part to read its card. Pick it again to see it alone. Drag a part out of place and let go: it springs back.

No ossicles: the source has no ear bones. The two inferior nasal conchae count as bones; the upper conchae belong to the ethmoid. The third molars are not in the model.

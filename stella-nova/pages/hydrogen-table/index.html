ABOUT

Each tile is a flat cut through the nucleus. Bright shows where the electron is likely to be. The vertical axis is z.

Scroll down for more shells, to n = 10. "n max" sets the last shell.

The tiles do not share one scale. Each tile has its own size, about n² Bohr radii, and its own brightest point.

For complex orbitals, |ψ|² does not change when you turn about z. So the cut through z shows the full shape. The density of m and −m is the same.

A real orbital is a mix of +m and −m, with cos mφ or sin mφ. It has |m| nodal planes through z. Each real tile is cut through its lobes: the x–y plane when |m| = l, else a vertical plane.

Tap a tile to see it large, with its nodes, its energy, and the radial curve r²R².

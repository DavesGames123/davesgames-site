Make your own
  1. Get keys for World Labs (the world) and FAL (meshes and sound). Generation is paid per use, on your account.
  2. git clone https://github.com/neilsonnn/image-blaster, then cd image-blaster.
  3. Copy .env.example to .env and put your WORLD_LABS_API_KEY and FAL_KEY in it. The keys stay on your machine.
  4. Put one image in input/.
  5. Run claude in the folder and say blast it and confirm each step with me.
  6. The world lands in worlds/<name>/. Choose that folder above.

Before you publish a generated world, read the terms of your World Labs plan: on a free plan, World Labs keeps the rights to the output.

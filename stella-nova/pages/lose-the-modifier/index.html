Lose the Modifier

A weak modifier pads a word that already exists in a stronger form. Very tired is exhausted. Walked slowly is trudged or ambled. Due to the fact that is because. One exact word reads faster and says more.

Type a phrase in the box. The page finds it as you type: other word forms (walking slowly), the base word alone (tired), any intensifier in front of a known word (awfully tired), a strong word to see what it replaces (exhausted), and small typos. With nothing typed, the page plays the pairs by itself. Pick a family in the chips to play only that family.

Paste text marks every weak phrase it knows in your own paragraph. Tap a mark for the options. Fix all shows the paragraph with each mark replaced, ready to copy. A verb in another tense (walks slowly) is marked but not replaced: change the tense by hand.

Strong words are not always better. A precise word carries a shade of meaning (trudged is tired, ambled is relaxed), so read the example before you swap.

The data

All pairs, tags and example sentences were written for this page. The tags give the part of speech and the register: neutral, formal, casual, vivid or literary.

Gear

Five-speed manual

Constant mesh · synchromesh · layshaft

The input gear drives the layshaft all the time, and the layshaft turns a free gear on the main shaft for each speed. A shift slides one synchro sleeve onto the dog teeth of one free gear and locks it to the main shaft.

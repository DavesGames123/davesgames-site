MAP PROJECTIONS — data sources, licences and credits
=====================================================

Geography
---------
Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), public domain.
  - data/world.json: ne_50m_land (simplified, Douglas-Peucker 0.04 deg),
    ne_110m_land and ne_110m_admin_0_countries, from
    https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (geojson/), written by
    tools/build-data.py.
  - Country borders on the main map: ne_50m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land,
    read from ../ancient-earth/data/overlays.json (the copy that page
    ships), not duplicated here.

Globe image
-----------
NASA Blue Marble: Next Generation (Reto Stockli, NASA Earth Observatory),
public domain, read from ../ancient-earth/data/present/color-2k.jpg.

Formulas and facts
------------------
All projection code is written for this page. The formulas follow:
  - John P. Snyder, Map Projections: A Working Manual, USGS Professional
    Paper 1395 (1987), and Flattening the Earth (1993).
  - Charles F. F. Karney, Transverse Mercator with an accuracy of a few
    nanometers, J. Geodesy 85 (2011): the Kruger series to n^6.
  - Bojan Savric, Tom Patterson, Bernhard Jenny, The Equal Earth map
    projection, Int. J. Geographical Information Science (2018).
  - Bojan Savric, Bernhard Jenny, Tom Patterson, Dusan Petrovic, Lorenz
    Hurni, A polynomial equation for the Natural Earth projection,
    Cartography and Geographic Information Science (2011).
  - Arthur H. Robinson's table (1963), as printed by Snyder (1993).
  - T. Vincenty, Direct and inverse solutions of geodesics on the
    ellipsoid, Survey Review (1975).
tests/proj-ref.json holds reference values computed with PROJ 9.8.1
(https://proj.org, MIT licence) through pyproj 3.8.0; only the numbers
are stored, no PROJ code.

Data
  Default: IEX Exchange HIST (TOPS feed), real trades on the IEX exchange,
  regular session, one day behind (T+1). Data provided for free by IEX.
  Terms: https://www.iex.io/products/market-data-connectivity/hist-terms
  ("If you distribute ... IEX historical data, you must cite IEX as the
  source."). tools/snapshot.mjs reads it at deploy time into data/live/;
  data/sample/iex.json is a dated committed sample. IEX volume is only the
  IEX part of US volume.
  Not used, by their terms: S&P 500 and DJIA on FRED ("Reproduction ... in
  any form is prohibited"), Twelve Data free tier (redistribution needs a
  Redistribution Rights Add-On), Alpha Vantage (personal, non-commercial
  use), Yahoo, Stooq (no terms reachable; a script check blocks downloads).
  Live mode: the user's own free API key for Twelve Data, Alpha Vantage or
  Polygon. That data stays under the provider's terms. The page does not
  store, copy or send it anywhere.
  Offline fallback: synthetic series made by synth.js, labelled as such.
  They are not market data.

Not financial advice
  The page shows probabilistic model forecasts. The models see only past
  prices. They know no news, earnings or fundamentals. The page gives no
  buy or sell signals.

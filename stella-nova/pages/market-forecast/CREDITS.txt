Data
  Demo mode: synthetic series that the page makes with a stochastic model
  (synth.js). They are not market data and show no real company.
  Live mode: the user's own free API key for Twelve Data, Alpha Vantage or
  Polygon. The data stays under that provider's terms. The page does not
  store, copy or send it anywhere.

Not financial advice
  The page shows probabilistic model forecasts. The models see only past
  prices. They know no news, earnings or fundamentals. The page gives no
  buy or sell signals.

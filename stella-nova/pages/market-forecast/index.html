About Market Forecast

The forecasts come from Chronos, pretrained time-series models by Amazon Science. The model reads the recent prices as a sequence and gives quantiles of the future values: the 10-90 % band, the 25-75 % band and the median. It runs on this device with onnxruntime-web, on the GPU (WebGPU) when the browser has one, else on the CPU (WebAssembly).

  • Chronos-Bolt Tiny (9M parameters) is in the page (17.6 MB, weights stored as fp16). Bolt Mini (21M) and Chronos-2 Small (28M) load their official weights from Hugging Face on first use and keep them in the browser cache.
  • Chronos-2 can forecast several series together (group attention). In Analysis it reads all positions as one group, so each forecast sees the other tickers. It still gives one distribution per ticker; the page joins them with a Gaussian copula fitted to recent returns.
  • Analysis also backtests the model on recent history: at the same time of day on earlier sessions, it forecasts to the close and checks how often the realised close fell in the 80 % band, and its quantile loss against naive forecasts.

Not financial advice. These are probabilistic model outputs. The models know nothing about news, earnings or fundamentals. Markets can move far outside any band. The page gives no buy or sell signals.

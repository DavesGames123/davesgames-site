About

The 2D view is a skeletal formula: each corner and line end is a carbon atom, and carbons carry enough hydrogens to make four bonds. A solid wedge points toward you, a hashed wedge away. The 3D view shows the same atoms in space. Point at an atom or a bond in either view to light it in both. In 3D, the bond lengths and angles at that atom show.

Structures, names and 3D conformers: PubChem (NCBI, public domain). 2D layouts, generated conformers and properties: OpenChemLib (BSD-3). logP is the OpenChemLib estimate. Descriptions are written for this page.

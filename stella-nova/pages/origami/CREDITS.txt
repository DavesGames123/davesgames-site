Origami Simulator (web port) -- credits and licenses
=====================================================

This page is a web port of the origami desktop app (Rust, wgpu, winit). It
carries third-party work under the MIT license. The license text of each source
follows, as each repository requires.

1. Folding simulation
   Ported from Origami Simulator by Amanda Ghassaei.
   https://github.com/amandaghassaei/OrigamiSimulator
   Paper: A. Ghassaei, E. D. Demaine, N. Gershenfeld, "Fast, Interactive
   Origami Simulation using GPU Computation", Origami 7 (7OSME), 2018.
   Used in: sim.js, solver.js (the bar-and-hinge model, its constants, and the
   per-step passes, by way of the Rust port in src/sim/).

2. Crease patterns from origamicp by Nihat Garibli.
   https://github.com/nihatgaribli/origamicp
   Files: patterns/pleat.fold, patterns/miura.fold, patterns/miura_large.fold,
          patterns/vertex4.fold, patterns/vertex6.fold, patterns/vertex8.fold

3. Crease patterns from flat-folder by Jason S. Ku. Traditional models from
   the ORIPA data set (the FOLD file_author field names the source).
   https://github.com/origamimagiro/flat-folder
   Files: patterns/birdbase.fold, patterns/birdbase2.fold, patterns/boat.fold,
          patterns/crane.fold, patterns/house.fold, patterns/kabuto.fold,
          patterns/pig.fold, patterns/pinwheel.fold, patterns/sailboat.fold,
          patterns/yakko.fold

On load, every pattern is normalized into the centred unit square (see
patterns.js fromFoldText). The files themselves are unchanged copies.

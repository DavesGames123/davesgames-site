ABOUT

Drag in the diagram to draw a crease. The ends snap to vertices and to the grid. The folded pane folds the same pattern with a bar-and-hinge model: axial springs hold the edges, crease springs drive each fold to its target, and face springs hold each triangle's shape.

A vertex ringed in amber fails a local flat-foldability check (Maekawa, Kawasaki, or Big-Little-Big). The checks are necessary, not sufficient. Complex models such as the crane fold most of the way, then pass through themselves, because the simulator has no layer order.

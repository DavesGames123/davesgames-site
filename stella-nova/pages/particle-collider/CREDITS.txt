PARTICLE COLLIDER — credits and sources
=======================================

This page is an original work for davesgames.io (Stella Nova). It is a
teaching model of particle collisions seen in a barrel detector.

Geant4
------
The physics models of the transport engine (physics.js, transport.js) follow
the methods described in the Geant4 Physics Reference Manual and the Geant4
Application Developer guides (https://geant4.web.cern.ch/docs/): step-by-step
tracking with the step limited by physics processes and geometry boundaries,
the number-of-interaction-lengths method, production cuts set by range,
physics tables on a logarithmic energy grid, the Berger-Seltzer (Moller /
Bhabha) stopping power, Klein-Nishina Compton sampling, and a GFlash-like
fast shower for soft particles.

This page is not Geant4. It contains no Geant4 source code and no Geant4 data
tables, and it is not endorsed by the Geant4 Collaboration. The names
"Geant4" and "The Geant4 toolkit" are used here only to cite the published
documentation.

Other physics sources
---------------------
- Particle Data Group, Review of Particle Physics (2024): "Passage of
  particles through matter" (Bethe-Bloch, density effect, radiation length,
  Tsai formula, Highland formula, shower profile), particle masses and
  lifetimes, and the "Atomic and Nuclear Properties of Materials" tables
  (density, mean excitation energy, Sternheimer parameters, nuclear
  interaction lengths, critical energies) used in materials.js.
- R. M. Sternheimer and R. F. Peierls, Phys. Rev. B 3 (1971) 3681: the
  density-effect rules for PbWO4 and brass.
- ESTAR (NIST): the shape of the low-energy radiative stopping power.
- R. D. Field and R. P. Feynman, Nucl. Phys. B136 (1978) 1: independent
  fragmentation of jets.
- Published machine parameters (LHC Run 3 and Pb-Pb, Tevatron, LEP, and the
  HL-LHC, HE-LHC, FCC-hh, Higgs factory, linear collider, CLIC and muon
  collider design studies): the collision energies in generators.js. The
  energies marked "hypothetical" belong to no machine or plan.

Detector
--------
The detector dimensions are near those of a large LHC barrel detector with a
3.8 T solenoid, rounded and simplified. No experiment or organisation endorses this
page.

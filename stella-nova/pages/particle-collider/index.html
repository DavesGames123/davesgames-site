About

The accelerator side computes the beam optics, RF and ramp from formulas (accel.js). The detector side generates proton-proton events and moves every particle step by step through the detector materials: ionisation (Bethe-Bloch with the density effect), multiple scattering (Highland), bremsstrahlung, pair production, Compton scattering, photoelectric absorption, decays with real lifetimes, and a parametrised hadronic cascade. A 3.8 T solenoid bends the tracks; tracks are fitted from their silicon hits.

The physics models follow the Geant4 Physics Reference Manual and the Geant4 Application Developer guides, and the PDG review of particle physics. This page is not Geant4, contains no Geant4 code, and is not endorsed by the Geant4 Collaboration. Details in CREDITS.txt.

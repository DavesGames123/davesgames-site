About

Each event is a Monte Carlo simulation: the generator makes the hard process at the chosen energy, and the transport engine follows every particle step by step through silicon, lead tungstate, brass and iron in a 3.8 T field (ionisation, multiple scattering, bremsstrahlung, pair production, Compton scattering, decays, a parametrised hadronic cascade). Energies marked "design" are proposed machines; "hypothetical" ones exist on no plan.

The physics models follow the Geant4 Physics Reference Manual and Application Developer guides and the PDG review. This page is not Geant4, contains no Geant4 code and is not endorsed by the Geant4 Collaboration. See CREDITS.txt.

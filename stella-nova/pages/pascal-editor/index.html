Pascal Editor is a free, open-source 3D building editor (walls, rooms, levels, roofs, furniture) built with React Three Fiber and WebGPU. Copyright © 2026 Pascal Group Inc. It is licensed under the MIT License (upstream copy).

The editor below is built from the pinned upstream commit by build.sh and runs from this site. davesgames.io changed only how it is hosted: scenes are saved in your browser (IndexedDB) and not on a server, the navbar has New, Import and Export, and the third-party plugins of the hosted app are not included. 3D models and textures load from Pascal's public asset hosts, as in the upstream code. davesgames.io is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pascal Group Inc.

Your scene stays in this browser only. To keep it or move it, use Export in the editor navbar, or Settings > Save Build.

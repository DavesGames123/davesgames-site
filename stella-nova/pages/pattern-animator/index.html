About

This is the Pattern Designer in motion. The pattern program runs once and makes a list of vector shapes; then each frame moves those shapes. Board channels turn, zoom and move the whole board. Motif channels turn, grow, hide and colour each shape about its own centre, in a wave across the board. Field channels bend every line: twist, lens, wave, ripple and flow.

A channel runs a whole number of cycles in the loop, so the last frame leads back to the first with no seam. A linear curve on a turn, a wave or a colour cycle moves on at a steady rate; on a size or a strength it goes there and back.

A morph sweeps across the board: the shapes of the first design shrink away as the shapes of the second grow in, and shapes that match slide from one place to the other.

A slow device gets a lighter view: first fewer pixels, then a lower density. It comes back when the frames are fast again. The caption shows the density in use.

All patterns, motion and code here are original work for davesgames.io.

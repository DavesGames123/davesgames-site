Planet Collision page (stella-nova/pages/planet-collision) — credits

tex/earth.jpg, tex/earth_clouds.jpg, tex/moon.jpg, tex/mars.jpg,
tex/mercury.jpg, tex/venus.jpg
  Surface maps, equirectangular. Solar System Scope,
  https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: downscaled from 2048 x 1024 to 1024 x 512 (Lanczos) and saved
  as JPEG quality 84; earth_clouds.jpg converted to one grey channel.
  Source files 2k_earth_daymap.jpg, 2k_earth_clouds.jpg, 2k_moon.jpg,
  2k_mars.jpg, 2k_mercury.jpg, 2k_venus_atmosphere.jpg (downloaded
  2026-10-11). The page makes smaller mip levels at run time.
  The proto-Earth, Theia, Ceres, Pluto, Charon and Vesta surfaces are
  procedural (shaders/common.wgsl fn planetAlbedo).

Colour maps (shaders/common.wgsl fn inferno, fn viridis)
  Polynomial fits of matplotlib's "inferno" and "viridis" colour maps
  (matplotlib colour maps by Nathaniel Smith and Stefan van der Walt,
  CC0), fit by Matt Zucker (shadertoy "Matplotlib colormaps", CC0).

Black-body colour (shaders/common.wgsl fn blackbody)
  The curve fit of the Planck locus in sRGB by Tanner Helland (2012),
  "How to convert temperature (K) to RGB".

Rendering method
  Screen-space fluid rendering: W. J. van der Laan, S. Green and M. Sainz,
  "Screen space fluid rendering with curvature flow", I3D 2009. This page
  uses the depth splat and a bilateral filter, not curvature flow.

Sky, atmosphere and bloom
  Adapted from stella-nova/pages/roche-limit (shaders/planet.wgsl fs_sky,
  fs_atmo; shaders/post.wgsl copied unchanged apart from its header).

Science
  data.js SOURCES lists the papers for the equation of state, the bodies
  and the impact presets.

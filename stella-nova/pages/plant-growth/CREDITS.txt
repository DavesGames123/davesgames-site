Plant Growth (stella-nova/pages/plant-growth)
==============================================

All code in this folder is our own. It uses ideas, one engine, scanned
textures and one library from the sources below.

1. The tree model (grow.js)
   W. Palubicki, K. Horel, S. Longay, A. Runions, B. Lane, R. Mech,
   P. Prusinkiewicz. "Self-organizing tree models for image synthesis".
   ACM Transactions on Graphics 28(3), Proc. SIGGRAPH 2009.
   Our own code from the description in the paper: buds and metamers, the
   shadow-propagation light grid, the Borchert-Honda resource allocation
   (M. Borchert, H. Honda 1984), the optimal growth direction, tropisms,
   branch shedding, and the pipe model (K. Shinozaki et al. 1964). Our
   additions: leaves add their light to the sums, a soft node budget, a
   hydraulic height limit, weeping shoots, fronds for the fern, and the
   calendar of seasons (season.js).

3. Leaf scans (assets/leaf-color.jpg, leaf-alpha.png, leaf-normal.jpg)
   ambientCG, https://ambientcg.com, CC0 1.0 (public domain).
   LeafSet016 (oak), LeafSet010 (maple), LeafSet024 (birch), LeafSet005
   (cherry), LeafSet023 (azalea), LeafSet019 (spruce sprays), 1K-JPG sets.
   tools/build-atlas.py cut each leaf out by its opacity map, turned it so
   the petiole points down, scaled it into a 256 px cell and set its mean
   colour. The willow leaf, the fern pinna, the cherry blossom, the azalea
   flower and the cotyledons are our own drawings in the same script.

4. Bark and ground (assets/bark-*.jpg, assets/ground-*.jpg)
   Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com, CC0 1.0 (public domain).
     bark-oak ...... Bark Brown 02 (Rob Tuytel)
     bark-maple .... Trident Maple Bark (Charlotte Baglioni)
     bark-spruce ... Pine Bark (Dimitrios Savva)
     bark-willow ... Bark Willow (Dario Barresi, Dimitrios Savva)
     bark-cherry ... Sakura Bark (Charlotte Baglioni)
     bark-bush ..... Jolcham Oak Bark 01 (Charlotte Baglioni), squashed 2:1
     ground-grass .. Forrest Ground 01 (Rob Tuytel)
     ground-meadow . Leafy Grass (Charlotte Baglioni)
     ground-litter . Forest Leaves 02 (Rob Tuytel)
   1k diffuse and OpenGL normal maps, resized by tools/prep-textures.py
   (colour 1024 px, normal 512 px). bark-birch and bark-fern are drawn by
   that script (our own).

5. three.js r160 (the 3D render)
   https://threejs.org, MIT licence, Copyright 2010-2023 three.js authors.
   Loaded from stella-nova/vendor/three@0.160.0.

The soil of the cut-away is drawn at run time on a canvas by render.js.
The page fetches only its own files; it makes no other network calls.

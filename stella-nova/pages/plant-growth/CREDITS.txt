Plant Growth (stella-nova/pages/plant-growth)
==============================================

All code in this folder is our own. It uses ideas and one engine from the
sources below.

1. Space colonisation (the branches)
   A. Runions, B. Lane, P. Prusinkiewicz.
   "Modeling Trees with a Space Colonization Algorithm".
   Eurographics Workshop on Natural Phenomena, 2007.
   grow.js is our own code from the description in the paper: attractor
   points in a crown envelope, an influence radius, a kill distance, the
   mean direction to the attractors, and the pipe model for the radii.

canvas by render.js. The page makes no network calls.

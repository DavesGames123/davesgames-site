About this page

A coarse model with honest limits. It starts from known structures in the PDB and shows how a chain can reach them. It does not predict a structure from a sequence.

How it works

Each bead is the C-alpha atom of one residue. The chain moves by Langevin dynamics: the force from the energy V, a friction, and random kicks whose size the temperature sets.

The Go model

Bonds, angles and dihedrals keep the local shape of the native chain. Pairs that touch in the PDB structure attract. Every other pair only repels. So the native fold is the lowest energy by construction: the landscape is a funnel with almost no traps.

What it cannot do

  • No sequence beyond the contact map. Chignolin and CLN025, or WW and FiP35, are almost the same model.
  • No non-native attraction, so no misfolded traps (CheY), no aggregation, no P23H rhodopsin.
  • No water, no side chains, no hydrogen bonds, no membrane, no chaperones.
  • Energy and time units are not calibrated: T is in ε/kB, time in τ.

What it gets right, roughly: the order in which parts of a fold form, and that topology sets much of the folding rate. AlphaFold predicts a structure; it does not simulate the path to it. Folding a protein from physics alone, atom by atom, still needs special supercomputers and works only for small, fast folders.

Q and the free energy

Q is the fraction of native contacts that are formed. A histogram of Q at one temperature gives the free energy F(Q). Near Tm a two-state protein shows two wells, unfolded and native, with a barrier between them.

Levinthal's paradox

If each residue had three shapes and the chain tried them at random, a 100-residue protein would need far longer than the age of the universe. Real proteins fold in microseconds to seconds. The way out is the funnel: each native contact that forms lowers the energy, so the search is biased downhill.

The HP lattice model

Two letters, H (hydrophobic) and P (polar), on a grid. Each H-H contact that is not a bond lowers the energy by one. Finding the lowest energy is NP-hard in 2D and 3D. The benchmarks show the best energy known from decades of search methods; watch the long ones stall.

Pulling

A constant force F on the two ends, along the line between them. If ε is about 1 kcal/mol, 1 ε/Å is about 69 pN. That mapping is an assumption, not a calibration.

Native structures: RCSB PDB (CC0). The model, integrator and plots on this page are written for it; no code comes from OpenMM, GROMACS or SMOG.

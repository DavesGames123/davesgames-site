Reaction library
About

Each reaction class is a template with a map number on every atom, hydrogens included. Applying it moves only bonds and charges, so every atom of the reactants ends in exactly one product. The 3D change keeps each atom's identity: the bonds that break turn red and snap, the bonds that form grow green, and the reaction centre glows amber.

The motion is a schematic of what changes, made by easing atoms from the reactant shapes to the product shape. It is not a simulation of the reaction path, and the conditions are textbook conditions, not a lab procedure.

Structures and 3D conformers: PubChem (NCBI, public domain) through the Molecule Explorer library; other conformers, layouts and ID codes: OpenChemLib (BSD-3). Tree layout from the fishdraw page. Equations: MathJax with mhchem (Apache-2.0). Descriptions are written for this page.

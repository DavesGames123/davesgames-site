Retro Shelf

Games people really played

Each game on this shelf was released for free by the people who own it. We host the unchanged archive and run it in an open-source engine in your browser. Nothing to install.

Most of these games need a keyboard. On a phone or tablet, the DOS game has on-screen keys, and the adventure games work with touch (tap to walk and to use).

The freeware shelf

Press a card to play. Keyboard and gamepad work. The ✕ Exit button at the top right ends the game.

Bring your own disc

An emulator for games that you own. This page hosts no games and no BIOS files. Pick a dump of a game that you own from your own device. Your browser reads the file here; it is not uploaded and it stays on your device.

1. System

Only play games that you own, from a copy that you made yourself. In many countries a copy downloaded from the internet is not legal, even when you own the game. Save files stay in this browser. Super Nintendo and Mega Drive are not here: the only cores for them have non-commercial licences.

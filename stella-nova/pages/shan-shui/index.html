ABOUT

Shan shui (山水, "mountain water") is a style of Chinese painting. This scroll has no end. The page draws new mountains, trees, houses, boats, and people as you travel.

Each part is a set of brush strokes made from noise and polygons. There is no image data. A seeded random generator makes each seed give the same scroll.

About

The Smith chart is the plane of the reflection coefficient Γ. The center is Γ = 0, a matched load. The rim is |Γ| = 1, where all the power reflects. The left end is a short circuit. The right end is an open circuit.

The cyan circles are constant resistance r. The arcs are constant reactance x. The upper half is inductive, and the lower half is capacitive. The pink grid is the admittance y = 1/z at the same point.

A length of lossless line does not change |Γ|. It only turns the point about the center. Half a wavelength is one full turn, so a λ/4 line changes z to 1/z.

ABOUT

The Smith chart is the plane of the reflection coefficient Γ. The center is Γ = 0, a matched load. The rim is |Γ| = 1, where all the power reflects. The left end is a short circuit. The right end is an open circuit.

The cyan circles are constant resistance r. The arcs are constant reactance x. The upper half is inductive, and the lower half is capacitive.

The pink grid is the same grid turned by 180°. It gives the admittance y = 1/z at the same point. Shunt parts add in y, so a stub match is easy to see on it.

A length of lossless line does not change |Γ|. It only turns the point on a circle about the center. Half a wavelength is one full turn, so a λ/4 line changes z to 1/z.

A stub match first moves along the line until g = 1. The stub then cancels the susceptance, and the point goes along the g = 1 circle to the center.

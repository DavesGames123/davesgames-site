    About the data and the model

    Each line is a transition of an electron from an upper level k to a lower level i. The photon carries the energy difference:

    “NIST intensity” shows the relative intensity of NIST (or gA when NIST gives none), as a log rank. “Boltzmann at T” uses an optically thin gas in local equilibrium:

    Each spectrum is scaled to its own strongest line. The page does not balance I against II (no Saha equation). A line with no Aki uses its NIST rank. Wavelengths are in air. The colours come from the CIE 1931 functions (fit of Wyman, Sloan and Shirley 2013); lines below 380 nm (UV) and above 750 nm (IR) are dashed in muted colours.

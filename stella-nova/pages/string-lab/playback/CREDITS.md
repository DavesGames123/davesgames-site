# String Lab playback: track credits

Every MIDI file in `tracks/` is our own work. We wrote each score by hand
in `tools/string-lab/scores.mjs` and made the files with
`node tools/string-lab/make-tracks.mjs`. No edition, engraving, recording
or MIDI file of another person was copied. The compositions are in the
public domain (the composers died more than 100 years ago, or the tunes are
traditional). Our transcriptions and arrangements are released under CC0.

| File | Work | Composer | What we wrote |
|---|---|---|---|
| romanza.mid | Romanza (Spanish Romance) | Anonymous, 19th century | Opening 16 bars, melody, inner notes and bass |
| greensleeves.mid | Greensleeves | Traditional English | Tune with a bass note on each bar |
| bourree.mid | Bourrée in E minor, BWV 996 | J. S. Bach (1685-1750) | Bars 1-4 twice, melody with a simplified bass |
| minuet.mid | Minuet in G, BWV Anh. 114 | Attributed to Christian Petzold (1677-1733) | Melody, bars 1-16 |
| ode-to-joy.mid | Ode to Joy (Symphony no. 9, finale theme) | Ludwig van Beethoven (1770-1827) | Melody in D |
| amazing-grace.mid | Amazing Grace, tune "New Britain" | Traditional American (1829 tune) | Melody in G |
| pop-g.mid | I-V-vi-IV in G | Our own exercise | Strummed chords |
| blues-e.mid | 12-bar blues shuffle in E | Our own exercise | Shuffle on the bass strings |
| travis-g.mid | Fingerpicking pattern in G | Our own exercise | Alternating-bass picking |

The arrangements are simplified for teaching. They are not urtext editions.

Uploaded files stay in the browser of the person who uploads them. The page
does not send them anywhere.

- 2026-10-17 DIAGRAM -> MATH: parseIndexTex rejects a bracket that spans two factors (`g_{a[c} R_{d]b}`, `\partial_{[\lambda} F_{\mu\nu]}`; Weyl and the Maxwell Bianchi identity need it). - 2026-10-17 MATH -> DIAGRAM: done. A bracket over several factors is on the Term: `term.sym = [{ type: 'sym'|'antisym', slots: [{ factor, index }], refs: [[factor, index], ...] }]` (`refs` is your pair form). `factor` is the place in `term.factors`; `index` is a position in `factorIndices(f)` = `f.indices` then `f.deriv` (so `index >= f.indices.length` is a derivative leg). A bracket inside one factor stays on `factor.sym = [{ type, slots: [index, ...] }]` in the same index space; `\partial_{[\lambda} F_{\mu\nu]}` is one factor F with `deriv: [lambda]` and `sym: [{ type: 'antisym', slots: [2, 0, 1] }]`. Draw both fields. `expandBrackets(expr)` writes every bracket out as a sum of permuted terms.

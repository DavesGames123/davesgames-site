Thermal Convection — credits and sources
========================================

The simulation, the views, the screensaver and this page are original work
for davesgames.io. No third-party simulation code is used.

Sources cited in the explainer (explainer.js REFS)
--------------------------------------------------
[1] H. Bénard (1900). Les tourbillons cellulaires dans une nappe liquide.
    Revue générale des sciences pures et appliquées 11, 1261–1271 and
    1309–1328.
[2] Lord Rayleigh (1916). On convection currents in a horizontal layer of
    fluid, when the higher temperature is on the under side. Philosophical
    Magazine (6) 32, 529–546. https://doi.org/10.1080/14786441608635602
[3] J. R. A. Pearson (1958). On convection cells induced by surface
    tension. Journal of Fluid Mechanics 4, 489–500.
    https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022112058000616
[4] S. Chandrasekhar (1961). Hydrodynamic and Hydromagnetic Stability.
    Oxford: Clarendon Press. Chapter II (onset values Ra_c = 1707.76 for
    two rigid walls, 1100.65 for a rigid bottom and a free top).
[5] Å. Nordlund, R. F. Stein and M. Asplund (2009). Solar surface
    convection. Living Reviews in Solar Physics 6, 2.
    https://doi.org/10.12942/lrsp-2009-2

Only facts from these sources are used (numbers, mechanisms, history). No
text, figures or data are copied from them.

Illustrative values
-------------------
The grid sizes, the burner patch, the top cooling law of the sun mode and
the effective Marangoni number are chosen to give a clear picture in real
time. They are not fitted to any one fluid.

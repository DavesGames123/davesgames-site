Maxwell, Heaviside, and the language of forms

Twenty to four

20Maxwell, 1865→ 4Heaviside, 1884→ 2forms

The same physics, written three times. Each step finds a better language for the geometry: circulation round small loops, flux out of small boxes, and finally the boundary of a boundary.

The field on the stage is the exact field of an oscillating point dipole in empty space, computed for every pixel. The formulas are typeset with MathJax.

Source: J. C. Maxwell, “A dynamical theory of the electromagnetic field”, Phil. Trans. R. Soc. 155 (1865), part III, equations A to H.

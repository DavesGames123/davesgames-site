VIRUS ATLAS - data credits
==========================

Every file in this folder is derived from an entry of the Protein Data Bank
(wwPDB, https://www.wwpdb.org; downloaded from RCSB PDB, https://www.rcsb.org).
PDB data are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). We credit
each entry and its primary citation below.

What the files keep: one bead per residue (the CA atom of an amino acid, the
P atom of a nucleotide, the C1 atom of a sugar), model 1 only, rounded to
0.1 A; the chain and entity names; the operators of the first biological
assembly (or, for TMV and the fibrils, a screw fitted from the deposited
model). tools/build-data.py makes them from the mmCIF files.

Illustrations made by the page, not in any PDB entry: the membranes, the
spike stalks and spike layouts of the two virions, the extra fibril layers
and rod subunits beyond the deposited ones (made with the fitted screw),
and every build, peel, explode, slice and sway animation.

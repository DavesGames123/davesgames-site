WEATHER MAP — data sources, licences and credits
================================================

Globe imagery (tex/, made by tools/fetch-earth.mjs; sizes, sha256 and the
source of each file are in tex/manifest.json)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - tex/day-8k.jpg, tex/day-2k.jpg, tex/us-day.jpg: NASA Blue Marble: Next
    Generation, July 2004, 21600x10800, no shaded relief (Reto Stockli,
    NASA Earth Observatory, from MODIS data). NASA imagery is in the
    public domain; NASA requests credit. us-day.jpg is a crop at the
    native 60 px/deg resolution (lat 15..60, lon -170..-50).
    https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/july/world.200407.3x21600x10800.jpg
    https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/BlueMarble
  - tex/night-4k.jpg: NASA Black Marble 2016 colour composite (NASA Earth
    Observatory, Suomi NPP VIIRS, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center).
    Public domain, credit requested. Moonlit land and sea are faded out,
    so the file holds city lights only.
    https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg
    https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/NightLights
  - tex/height-4k.png: Blue Marble Next Generation topography, from the
    GEBCO 08 grid (NASA Earth Observatory). Public domain, credit
    requested. Grey, 0 = sea; approximate metres = 6500 * (v/255)^(1/0.85).
    https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/topography/gebco_08_rev_elev_21600x10800.jpg
  - tex/water-2k.png: sea from Blue Marble Next Generation bathymetry
    (GEBCO 08, NASA Earth Observatory, public domain), plus lakes from
    Natural Earth 10m lakes v5.1.2 (https://www.naturalearthdata.com),
    public domain.
    https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bathymetry/gebco_08_rev_bath_5400x2700.jpg
    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_10m_lakes.geojson

Live weather data
-----------------
  - Forecasts and the temperature grid: Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com).
    Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licence CC BY 4.0
    (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Open-Meteo builds its
    forecasts from national weather services (NOAA GFS/HRRR, DWD, ECMWF
    and others).
  - Fronts, highs and lows: NOAA National Weather Service, Weather
    Prediction Center (WPC) coded surface bulletin
    (https://www.wpc.ncep.noaa.gov/). Work of the US federal government,
    public domain.
  - NWS alerts and observations (https://www.weather.gov,
    https://api.weather.gov): public domain.
  - Radar: RainViewer (https://www.rainviewer.com), weather radar tiles
    from the RainViewer public API, used with the credit line
    "Radar data: RainViewer". RainViewer collects the radar from national
    weather services (NOAA NEXRAD in the United States).

The idea of an infinite, walkable city made by wave function collapse is from marian42/wavefunctioncollapse by Marian Kleineberg (MIT, Unity). The algorithm is WaveFunctionCollapse by Maxim Gumin (MIT).

No code, models, module set or look come from those projects. The module set, the geometry, the palette, the solver (Rust, WebAssembly) and the WebGPU renderer are our own.
